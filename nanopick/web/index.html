How this works

1. Say what you remember. Any words from the file's name. “receipt last” finds receipt-november-last.jpg. Fewer words finds more.

2. Narrow it down. Roughly what kind, roughly when. You can order by newest, biggest or name.

3. Click a result to see it right here — pictures show on the page.

4. Do the thing: open it, show the folder it lives in, send a copy to your project folder, or tick a few and pack them into one zip.

The promise: nanoPick never moves, renames or deletes your files. Copies only, into the folder you chose, and nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.
